# Certificates (2025) Root CA Certificate
This is the public root certificate for Akuzo's internal PKI.

The certificate (ca.crt) and its public key are published here for anyone to download and trust at their own discretion.

No license is required to use or distribute the public certificate, as public keys and X.509 certificates are not subject to copyright. You are free to copy, distribute, and install this certificate in your trust stores.

**Use at your own risk. Trusting this CA gives it the ability to issue certificates for any domain or identity.**
